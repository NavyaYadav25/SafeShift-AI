# SafeShift AI Demo Script

## Opening (20 Seconds)

Imagine it is 2:00 AM.

A critical production service suddenly crashes.

Traditionally, an engineer receives an alert, wakes up, checks logs, investigates the issue, estimates impact and manually prepares a fix.

This process can take hours.

SafeShift AI reduces that response time by automating investigation and recovery planning while keeping humans in control.

---

## Step 1: Trigger Incident (15 Seconds)

Click:

Generate Critical Incident

A simulated production outage is created.

The system immediately records the incident and begins analysis.

---

## Step 2: AI Investigation (25 Seconds)

SafeShift AI launches multiple agents.

The agents:

* Analyze the exception.
* Investigate root causes.
* Evaluate severity.
* Estimate business impact.

The dashboard displays:

* Root cause
* Severity score
* Priority level
* Customer impact
* Revenue risk

---

## Step 3: AI Recovery Planning (20 Seconds)

The system automatically generates:

* Recovery steps
* Recommended actions
* Risk assessment
* Confidence score

This reduces manual troubleshooting effort.

---

## Step 4: AI Patch Recommendation (20 Seconds)

SafeShift AI generates:

* Original code snippet
* Suggested fix
* Confidence estimate

Engineers can review exactly what the AI proposes.

---

## Step 5: Human Approval (15 Seconds)

Unlike fully autonomous systems, SafeShift AI keeps humans in control.

The engineer reviews the recommendation and clicks:

Approve Fix

---

## Step 6: Deployment Validation (20 Seconds)

After approval, SafeShift AI simulates:

* Patch deployment
* Automated testing
* Health checks
* Service recovery

This creates a transparent recovery workflow.

---

## Closing (20 Seconds)

Most monitoring platforms stop at alerting.

SafeShift AI continues through:

Alert → Investigation → Diagnosis → Fix → Validation → Approval

helping engineering teams reduce downtime, improve reliability and respond faster to production incidents.
