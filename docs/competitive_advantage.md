# Competitive Advantage

## Existing Tools

- Datadog
- New Relic
- Grafana
- PagerDuty

These tools focus mainly on monitoring, alerts, and observability.

## Gap in Existing Solutions

Most tools tell teams that something is broken.

Few tools explain:

- Business impact
- Revenue risk
- Customer impact
- Recovery prioritization
- AI-assisted response workflow

## SafeShift AI Advantage

SafeShift AI goes beyond monitoring.

It combines:

- Incident investigation
- Root cause analysis
- Business impact estimation
- Recovery planning
- Human approval workflow

within a single AI-driven incident response platform.

## Human-in-the-Loop Design

SafeShift AI never deploys fixes automatically.

Human operators remain in control of all critical decisions.
