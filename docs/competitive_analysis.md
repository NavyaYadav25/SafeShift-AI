## Differentiator

Existing platforms focus primarily on monitoring and alerting.

SafeShift AI focuses on autonomous incident investigation and resolution assistance.

Instead of only notifying engineers about problems, SafeShift AI helps them move from:

Alert → Investigation → Diagnosis → Fix → Validation → Approval

within a single workflow.

---

## Unique Insight

The biggest delay during production incidents is not alert generation.

The delay occurs between:

* Detecting the incident
* Understanding the root cause
* Determining the safest recovery action

SafeShift AI reduces this gap using a coordinated multi-agent workflow while preserving human control.
