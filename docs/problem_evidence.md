# Problem Evidence

## Why This Problem Matters

Modern applications run on complex cloud infrastructure consisting of APIs, databases, microservices, and third-party integrations.

When an incident occurs:

- Engineers spend valuable time investigating logs and alerts.
- Teams struggle to understand business impact quickly.
- Critical incidents may not be prioritized correctly.
- Downtime can lead to revenue loss and poor user experience.

## Observations

Common challenges faced by engineering teams:

1. Root cause analysis takes too long.
2. Alert fatigue caused by excessive notifications.
3. Lack of visibility into business impact.
4. Manual recovery coordination.
5. Delayed decision-making during critical incidents.

## Expected Benefits of SafeShift AI

- Faster incident diagnosis
- Reduced Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR)
- Better prioritization of incidents
- Improved operational efficiency
- Human-supervised AI decision making
